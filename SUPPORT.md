# Support

Thanks for using **АТМОСФЕРА**! Here is how to get help.

## How to get support

- **Bug reports & feature requests** — open an issue using one of the templates
  in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/bestdeejay-design/arental_2/issues).
- **Security issues** — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); report privately to
  **hello@arental.ru**.
- **Business / collaboration** — email **hello@arental.ru** or call
  **+7 812 928 74 78**.

## Before you ask

1. Search [existing issues](https://github.com/bestdeejay-design/arental_2/issues)
   to see if your question is already answered.
2. Read the [README](README.md) — most setup and usage questions are covered
   there.

## Response time

This is a community-supported project. We do our best to respond within a few
business days, but responses are not guaranteed.
